✅ Custom Dictionary (Words to Accept)

How to use the custom dictionary

  • Add one word per line (case-insensitive)
  • Lines starting with # are comments and will be ignored
  • Use this for persona names (Priya, Mereana, etc.)
  • Add NZ place names, surnames, and technical terms
  • Changes take effect immediately when saved

Note: Common NZ English spellings (colour, metre, etc.) are built into the system and don't need to be added here.

⚠️ Suspicious Words (Words to Always Flag)

What are suspicious words?

Suspicious words are technically valid English words that commonly appear as typos in persona files. These words will always trigger a warning when detected, even though they're in the dictionary.

  • Example: "dunner" (valid word) is often a typo for "dinner"
  • Add one word per line (case-insensitive)
  • You can add inline comments after the word with #
  • Changes take effect immediately when saved
